What the curves mean

  • Actual (solid orange) — average MW from curated.generation_actual for the selected zone, type, and period.
  • Historical mean (dashed blue) — seasonal baseline computed from all prior years (excluding the displayed year):
    • Daily series → average of daily means at the same calendar position, ±7-day smoothing window across prior years.
    • 24h profile → average of hourly values at the same hour-of-day within the same month, across prior years.
    • Monthly series → average of daily means within the same calendar month, across prior years.
  • P5–P95 corridor (light blue shaded area) — 5th–95th percentile range of the same historical samples. Captures the "normal" working range and trims rare outliers.
  • Wind = sum of Wind Onshore (B19) + Wind Offshore (B18). For the corridor in Wind mode, percentiles are added component-wise (slight overestimate of band width; for tighter bounds inspect Wind Onshore / Offshore separately).
Time zone: CET · Unit: MW (average power per hour) · Source: ENTSO-E Transparency / local Postgres
